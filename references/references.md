## Connection to the Research Topic

Together, these references provide the literature base for examining how **citation prominence, English-language dominance, database coverage, recommender-system popularity, citation searching, active learning, and generative AI** may influence the visibility and retrieval of scholarly literature.

The central concern of the research paper is whether these mechanisms can contribute to **unequal recall**, particularly for relevant papers that are less cited or published in languages other than English.
